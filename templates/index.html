Research Study Overview

IMPACT S2S (IMPlementing Action for tobacco smoking Cessation Treatment: Strategies 2 Scale) is a research study being conducted as part of the Johns Hopkins ALACRITY Center for Health & Longevity in Mental Illness. This study is being done to improve the delivery of tobacco smoking cessation treatment to people living with serious mental illness.

In this brief survey, you will compare strategies in pairs and can also suggest new ideas.

Estimated completion time: about 10 minutes.

Your voice matters. Insights from this survey will inform how smoking cessation treatment is delivered to people living with serious mental illness in the future.

KEY INFORMATION

This research study is being done to improve the delivery of smoking cessation treatment to people living with serious mental illness (SMI).

Your participation will require completing a brief survey, comparing strategies to increase the delivery of smoking cessation.

PURPOSE

In this study, we will work with community partners to identify the best ways to implement smoking cessation programs in community mental health settings.

RISKS AND BENEFITS

The risks associated with this study are minimal. You may become bored or tired during the survey. You do not have to answer any questions you do not want to answer. You may not receive direct benefits from participating, but your responses will contribute to improving smoking cessation services for people with SMI.

VOLUNTARY PARTICIPATION

You do not have to agree to be in this study. Your consent is implied by participation. If you wish to stop, simply close the survey web page. Leaving this study will not stop you from getting regular medical care, or affect your employment/education.

IDENTIFIABLE INFORMATION

No personal identifiable information will be collected or stored. We will collect your role type (e.g. mental healthcare patient, healthcare provider/administrator) and your strategy preferences. Please refrain from sharing identifiable information in the free-text box. If you unintentionally input identifiable information, the study team will redact it from your response.

CONTACT INFORMATION

If you have any questions about this study, please feel free to contact the Principal Investigator Dr. Christina Yuan at 410-955-0510 or cyuan16@jhu.edu

The IRB can help you if you have questions about your rights as a research participant or if you have other questions, concerns or complaints about this research study. You may contact the IRB at 410-502-2092 or jhmeirb@jhmi.edu
