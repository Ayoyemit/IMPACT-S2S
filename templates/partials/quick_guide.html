We want to identify the best ways to implement smoking cessation treatment in community mental health settings. Understand the what, where, who, and how of smoking cessation treatment by expanding the cards below.

Smoking cessation treatment is an evidence-based practice that involves four activities. Hover over each to learn more.

📋 Assessing smoking status
This involves asking patients if they currently smoke tobacco and assessing the severity of tobacco dependence.
💬 Assessing willingness to quit
This involves asking patients whether they are thinking about trying to quit smoking and if they have a time goal.
🧠 Behavioral counseling
This involves counseling focused on smoking cessation, delivered by providers trained in motivational interviewing techniques.
💊 Pharmacotherapy
This involves the prescription and management of smoking cessation medications and/or nicotine replacement therapy, managed by a prescribing physician or nurse.
Community mental health centers provide a variety of services. These centers are generally where people with serious mental illness receive most of their healthcare.
Therapists, psychiatrists, and nurses at community mental health centers would become involved in the delivery of smoking cessation treatment, involving behavioral counseling and medication management.
